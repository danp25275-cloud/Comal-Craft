Our Story

About Comal Craft

Every tortilla begins with exceptional flour. We source heirloom-style organic wheat that is grown and milled in Texas and combine it with carefully selected ingredients to create tortillas with remarkable flavor, aroma, and texture.

Each batch is mixed, pressed, and cooked in small quantities to preserve the character of traditional flour tortillas.

Our goal is simple: bring authentic artisan tortillas back to the family table using honest ingredients with a modern twist.
